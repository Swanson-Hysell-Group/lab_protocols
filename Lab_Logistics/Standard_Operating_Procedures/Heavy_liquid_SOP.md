## 1. Set up
1. Grab all of the following:
- Your samples, obviously
- LST, which would be our heavy liquid. It should be in the corrosive cupboard. We usually use the “reclaimed” one, which is twice filtered and the same density (2.85 g/cm^3 ish) as LST should be. The “filtered” one has extra water and is less dense than it should be (and we need to boil the extra water out).
- Centrifuge tubes
- Plastic pipettes
- Filter paper (P8)
- Petri dishes
- Flasks and a large beaker
- Funnel
- Hot plate
- Centrifuge
- Squirt bottle of Ethyl Alcohol and distilled water

2. Wear plastic gloves if you want your hands to not get sticky
3. Fill the large beaker with some water and put the squirt bottle of distilled water in, then  put the beaker on the hot plate. Turn the hot plate on to 120C or something like that.
4. Idk where the balanced centrifuge tube with LST already in it usually is. Ask yiming
5. Fold the filter paper twice, make into a cone, then put it on a funnel, then put the funnel on a centrifuge tube. Pour ~25 mL LST in. Do this for as many samples as you have.

## 2. Centrifuge sample
1. Label the centrifuge tube.
2. Pour your sample in.
3. Put the centrifuge tubes in the centrifuge in a balanced way.
4. Start the centrifuge and let it run for a couple minutes.

## 3. Get the heavy grains out
1. Label filter paper. Fold it twice and make into a cone, then put it on the funnel, which is put on a flask. Ensure the filter paper is stuck together properly by squirting some water on it.
2. Label the petri dish cover. Add some water to the petri dish.
3. Use the pipette to get the heavy grains that have sunk to the bottom out. Squeeze the pipette as you go in the LST in the centrifuge tube so you don’t suck in the light grains that are floating. Swirl as you suck the bottom to ensure you get everything. Pipette this to the petri dish. Repeat if needed.
4. Dump out the remaining LST solution (that contains the lighter grains) in the centrifuge tube to the filter paper, so the LST gets filtered to the flask. Squirt the centrifuge tube with water and twirl it so everything gets washed out into the filter paper. 
5. Swirl the petri dish so the grains are in the middle. Pump the pipette a couple times in the side (at an angle so the grains continue to get centered) to clean it so all grains are washed out. Then pipette as much water out from the petri dish as you can to the filter (that goes to the flask). When you pipette to the filter, do it in a way such that the light grains are washed to the center. Repeat by adding water to the petri dish again to clean the grains from LST. Repeat as many times as you want. 
6. Add ethyl alcohol to the petri dish, and bring it over to the microscope.
7. Put the filter paper cone on the clamp in the fumehood to dry.
